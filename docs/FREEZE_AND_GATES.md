# Frozen project thesis; unfrozen evidence claims

**Project choice:** BANLAG — Regulatory Evidence Visibility. No more open-ended ideation while testing this project's decisive gates. Its central problem/target audience/API dependency are frozen; do not grow scope for feature count.

**Four externally observable layers:** Gazette exact notice → organic discoverability → independently captured cited sources → the actual search AI answer. This is a *comparison of observations*, not a reconstruction of Google's hidden retrieval/cause.

**Minimum heroic case:** A complete, verifiably covered formulation; strong present-tense current-use or availability wording; no contemporaneous mention of the Indian restriction; provenance and negative control. Stronger: same-query prominent organic result states the ban. Best: an AI-cited page demonstrably contains the exact restriction, but the AI output omits it.

**No artificial legal duty:** Google is not assumed legally obligated to mention a ban in every pharmacology answer. Missing status alone is not an incorrect claim. Reviewer must assess context.

## Gates, in hard order

1. **G1 preregistered public browser test:** exactly the original ten, >=3 positive exact-formulation current-use omissions in both captures, >=1 outside serratiopeptidase. Locked Pass A cannot be modified retrospectively. If failed, do not claim the phenomenon is common.
2. **G2 legal:** exact operative notification, scope, per-notice identity, date and non-exhaustive court check. No claim of global legal certainty, actual sale, medical danger, patient possession offense, or advice to stop treatment.
3. **G3 differentiator:** at least one **real** Tier 2/Tier 3 source visibility case or another significant demonstrable capability beyond GRAP's rulebook-vs-AI citation analysis; perform comparative task/control rather than benchmark feature count.
4. **G4 SerpApi:** only with explicit approval, <=3 potential billed calls for a first feasibility check. Verify India-specific parseable AI text, citations, search ID, timestamps and failure modes. A public-Google screenshot isn't a SerpApi capture.
5. **G5 engineering:** real capture replay tests, negative controls, full legal & medical copy review, keyless clean install, local <3-min working demo, documentation, honest source/AI disclosures. No fake superiority metrics.
6. **G6 submission:** user's explicit approval before commits, public repo, video upload, deployment or submission; verify links in incognito and submit within the official window.

**Highest originality risk:** GRAP Audit has sophisticated rule-versus-AI comparison plus citation attribution; BANLAG's new drug vertical and exact matching do not automatically surpass it. A unique evidence-tier feature is a design hypothesis until it catches a case GRAP's engine cannot readily reproduce.

## Do NOT build now

- Full 2018/2024 drug corpus, OCR, claims about real medicine sales, brand/seller accusation, broad multilingual audits, monitoring scheduler, agent chatbots, payment/booking integration, public patient search, extra API engines solely to raise counts.

## Demo narrative (requires real evidence)

0–7s: official gazette composition and legal scope. 7–16s: real SerpApi search response and AI statement, with search ID and capture date. 16–25s: organic and *actual cited-page* evidence visibility tier. 25–32s: near-identical legal look-alike → confidently NOT FLAGGED / UNKNOWN. End with one auditable dossier. Negative or absent results must be shown honestly.
