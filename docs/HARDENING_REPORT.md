## 5. Trust model (precise)

**Origins** (`banlag/provenance.py`), highest to lowest:

| Origin | Who can create it | Proves | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TRUSTED_LOCAL_SERPAPI_CAPTURE` | only `banlag.collector` on this machine, after explicit plan-hash approval | these exact raw bytes were received from serpapi.com by this collector with these params; provider `search_metadata` (id, created_at, processed_at, status, json_endpoint) is bound to the raw bytes; record sealed with HMAC-SHA256 under a machine-local key | Google's internal reasons; what any person saw; the truth or current content of any cited page. SerpApi supplies no signature, so this is a local process attestation, not a provider attestation |
| `ARCHIVED_SERPAPI_CAPTURE` | the same record on a machine without the key | raw bytes and recorded metadata are mutually consistent | that this collector produced it |
| `BROWSER_ARCHIVE_HASH_BOUND` | `evidence_ingest` from the locked G1 archive | the transcription is byte-identical to the hash-locked archive made under a locked preregistration | it is not a SerpApi capture; no provider IDs; source chips only (no URLs) |
| `USER_SUPPLIED_UNVERIFIED` | any import (UI/API), whatever `source_kind` says | nothing about origin; metadata consistency is reported, never trusted | — |
| `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE` | tests and demo samples | nothing | — |

**Tiers** (`banlag/engine.py`, all *candidates*; human + legal review gates any publication):

- **T1 observed omission** — preregistered label E on this surface: exact set established in the AI text's lead sentence, a current-use claim bound to it, no India restriction bound to it. Strength STRONG (prescribing, dosing/instructions, availability, patient-addressed) or WEAK (present-tense "is used to").
- **T2 same-search discoverability** — T1 + an organic result from the same search (same response, or a page_token parent link verified by the collector) whose own sentence states the exact restriction. Never "the model retrieved it".
- **T3 cited-source contradiction** — T1 + a page cited by *that* current-use claim (same surface, `reference_indexes`) whose locally snapshotted text states the exact restriction. Imported page text never counts.
- `verified_evidence_tier` = tier only for `TRUSTED_LOCAL_SERPAPI_CAPTURE` (seal verified with the local key). Hash-consistent archives get `replay_evidence_tier` and status `ARCHIVE_REPLAY_ORIGIN_UNAUTHENTICATED`, never a verified tier (Codex P1-3); imports and synthetic fixtures are always 0.
- Tier 2/3 restriction statements must be current and Indian, judged per restriction EVENT (`semantics.sentence_events` / `event_qualifies`, shared with label D, the status capture and the UI) on ONE event bound to the exact named set, with a SUPPORTED_EXACT composition for the whole evidence text (V0.2 text below kept for history): negated, conditional, revoked/stayed/set-aside (also by a later sentence), historical or dated before 2026, proposed/draft, foreign (place or authority attached to the predicate) or jurisdiction-unspecified restrictions never qualify, nor does a sentence whose formulation is altered or variable (Codex P1-4/5; second audit). An Indian government host supplies jurisdiction only to a sentence that itself states an unqualified restriction. A sealed capture proves what SerpApi returned, not that the statement is legally true.

**Publication gate** (from the legal audit): withhold on unverified origin, non-exact or contested composition, capture before 20 Jun 2026 or with unverified time, incomplete capture, or legal status older than 7 days; spelling-variant matches require reviewer confirmation; human review always required.

**Server**: binds 127.0.0.1; refuses foreign `Host` (DNS rebinding) and cross-site POSTs; JSON only, ≤2 MB, ≤64 levels; screenshots served only if hash-identical to the records of a corpus that verified against the pinned anchor on this load; strict CSP; never imports the collector or any network client (AST-checked in tests).
